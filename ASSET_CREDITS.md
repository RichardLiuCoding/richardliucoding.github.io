# Figure sources: visual revision

## Figures provided by Richard Liu

All three files are copied byte-for-byte from the supplied website reference folder. Scientific panels, labels, scales, and data are unchanged. The website displays complete images and provides full-size links.

- `assets/images/combinatorial-discovery.png`: source `Fig-2.png`. Multi-objective measurements, Gaussian-process predictions, and acquisition maps across an (Al, Sc, B)N library. Related article: https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.78139 .
- `assets/images/structure-property-learning.png`: source `fig1.png`. Images and measured objectives feed deep kernel learning models and acquisition decisions. Related preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08073 .
- `assets/images/genetic-learning-workflow.png`: source `fig-TOC.png`. Genetic candidate generation, feature extraction, surrogate prediction, and experimental verification. No unverified publication citation is assigned.

These author-supplied assets retain their source authorship and applicable rights. They are not assigned the licenses of the different figures used in the first website package.
