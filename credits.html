Multi-objective characterization of a composition library

Research figure supplied by Richard Liu as Fig-2.png, reproduced intact. The figure connects measured responses to Gaussian-process predictions and acquisition maps across an (Al, Sc, B)N library.

Multi-objective structure–property learning

Research workflow figure supplied by Richard Liu as fig1.png, reproduced intact. It connects image patches, measured objectives, deep kernel learning, and selection of the next experimental location.

Related preprint: Domain Switching on the Pareto Front: Multi-Objective Deep Kernel Learning in Automated Piezoresponse Force Microscopy.

Genetic search and learned priorities

Method schematic supplied by Richard Liu as fig-TOC.png, reproduced intact. It illustrates candidate generation by a genetic algorithm and prioritization by deep kernel learning. DNA motifs represent candidate encodings.
