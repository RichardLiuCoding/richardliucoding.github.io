Lab & tools

Building an experimental
platform for discovery.

Our planned laboratory brings together scanning probe instruments, programmable control, and machine learning. Instrument choices will follow the scientific measurements each project requires.

Instrumentation roadmap

The capabilities below describe planned purchases and development priorities. Specific instrument models, installation dates, and operational availability have not yet been announced.

Planned capability

Atomic-scale fabrication
and characterization

A scanning probe platform for local structural and electronic measurements, with a path toward automated atomic manipulation.

Scientific purpose: connect controlled changes in atomic structure with the resulting material response.

Planned capability

Automated scanning probe microscopy

Flexible imaging and spectroscopy with programmable acquisition, instrument tuning, and repeat measurements.

Scientific purpose: explore local functional response and test whether automated measurements are reproducible across samples and settings.

Planned capability

Connected characterization tools

Complementary measurements linked through consistent sample identifiers and experimental records. The specific methods will depend on the materials and physical questions.

Scientific purpose: compare observations from different probes and connect local measurements with broader material behavior.

Development priority

Software for closed-loop experiments

Instrument interfaces, experiment planning, data analysis, and records that connect an experimental decision to its measured outcome.

Scientific purpose: let models guide experiments while preserving the information needed to inspect and reproduce each result.
